Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 Explains how Fanfares handles information

This Privacy Policy explains how Fanfares collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit the website or use related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email address, or phone number.
  • Trip and booking information submitted by organisers or fans.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device information, and usage logs.

2. How we use information

  • To run the service and show trip details to the right users.
  • To send service updates, confirmations, and operational messages.
  • To improve the website, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • To comply with legal or regulatory obligations where required.

3. Sharing information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Fanfares, with organisers and fans as needed to provide the service, or where disclosure is required by law.

4. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how the site is used, and improve performance.

5. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. You can also object to certain processing in some circumstances.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

Fanfares is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to consent to digital services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your information, please update this section with your support email before launch.
